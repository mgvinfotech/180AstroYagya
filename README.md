### 1.  Vedic Frontend
- **Color Palette & Visuals**: Sacred Temple Mahogany (`#170709`), Mystic Obsidian (`#070203`), Radiant Vedic Gold (`#D4AF37`, `#FFD700`), and Royal Vermillion.

### 2. Comprehensive Admin Sanctuary
Manage 100% of the portal without touching code:
- **📊 Sanctuary Dashboard**: Real-time stats on incoming inquiries, pending bookings, active services, astrologers, and recent leads.
- **🖼️ Hero Sliders**: Create, edit, reorder, and activate/deactivate homepage rotating banners.
- **🧭 Dynamic Navigation Menus**: Manage Header navigation, parent-child dropdowns, footer quick links, and badges ("Sacred", "Book Live").
- **🗂️ Categories & Subcategories**: Manage Vedic taxonomy for Yagyas, Jyotish, Graha Shanti, and Meditation.
- **🕉️ Services & Yagyas**: Full CRUD for rituals including Dakshina in INR & USD, priest count, duration, consecrated mantra chanted, samagri details, and spiritual benefits bullet list builder.
- **🔮 Astrologers & Acharyas**: Manage scholar profiles, titles, qualifications, experience years, consultation fees, and specialties.
- **⭐ Devotee Reviews**: Manage customer ratings, verified badges, and miracle stories.
- **📬 Inquiries & Leads**: View submitted leads with complete Kundli birth data, update status (`pending`, `contacted`, `scheduled`, `completed`, `cancelled`), add internal notes, and initiate 1-click WhatsApp chats.
- **📧 Dynamic SMTP Settings**: Configure Mail Host, Port, Username, Password, Encryption, Sender Name/Email, and run **Live Test Email Dispatches**.
- **🌐 Social Media Links**: Manage official YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Twitter/X channels.
- **❓ Vedic FAQs**: Manage accordion questions and answers.
- **⚙️ Site Settings**: Global settings for Logo, Tagline, Header Top Alert notification, Ashram Address, Contact Phone, Toll-Free, Footer About, and SEO Meta tags.
